# Results index

Two eval paradigms, one results file each:

- [RESULTS_contrastive.md](RESULTS_contrastive.md) — A/B/C triplet invariant (d(A,B) < d(A,C), d(B,C)); pass-rate heatmaps across all 10 families × 4 models × flat/mean. Generated by `scripts/gen_results.py`.
- [RESULTS_continuous.md](RESULTS_continuous.md) — pairwise-continuous: ladder adjacency, i.e. whether each clip's two nearest embedding neighbors are its θ-adjacent clips. Scored by `evals.continuous.measure`; families in `evals/continuous/`.
