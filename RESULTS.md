# Results index

Two eval paradigms, one results file each:

- [RESULTS_contrastive.md](RESULTS_contrastive.md) — A/B/C triplet invariant (d(A,B) < d(A,C), d(B,C)); pass-rate heatmaps across all 10 families × 4 models × flat/mean. Generated by `scripts/gen_results.py`.
- [RESULTS_continuous.md](RESULTS_continuous.md) — pairwise-continuous: ladder adjacency, i.e. whether each clip's two nearest embedding neighbors are its θ-adjacent clips. Scored by `evals.continuous.measure`; families in `evals/continuous/`.

## Canonical eval set (2026-08-31)

The families that constitute the suite going forward (latest design version of each; everything else is legacy/superseded or a design study):

- **Fixed-scene contrastive** — bowl, cube v2, basic_color v2, basic_position, roll, occlusion v2
- **Sweep contrastive** — count3 (nested counting, constant gaps), bounce (void), deform (history), collision
- **Continuous** — translation, rotation

Readout set: flat (raw) vector per model, cos + L1, plus the V-JEPA predictor readout (and V-JEPA mean on continuous); FastWAM under the fixed prompt context. All canonical-family renders use all-intra encoding (`-g 1` in `evals/common/video.py`) from 2026-08-31 on — earlier-encoded families are unaffected where results are negatives (codec residue only inflates scores).
